# Flak Defense

A WebXR anti-aircraft battlefield game for Meta Quest (also playable on desktop and mobile).

**Play:** https://nag-fdestech.github.io/flak-defence/

- **Quest:** open the link in the Quest Browser and tap **Enter VR**. Point a controller and hold the trigger to fire. Flick the thumbstick to turn (click it for smooth turning). When reloading, grab the glowing clip from the rack with the grip button and push it into the loader (or press A/X and wait). B/Y recenters your seat.
- **Desktop:** click to aim with the mouse, hold to fire, R reloads.

Shoot down fighters, bombers, dive bombers, low-level attackers, paratroopers, tanks and an airship boss every 5 waves, across dusk, night, storm and dawn battles. Spend points in the supply depot between waves on upgrades and three guns (quad 20 mm, twin 40 mm, 88 mm flak). Friendly planes (blue markers) drop health, ammo and upgrade crates. Don't shoot them.

Everything (models, textures, sound) is generated in code; the only dependency is three.js from a CDN.
